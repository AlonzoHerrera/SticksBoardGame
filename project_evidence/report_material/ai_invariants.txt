AI Invariants

The following invariants describe the rules that must always remain true while using the AI architecture in Game B.

------------------------------------------------------------
Game Structure
------------------------------------------------------------

1. The game always contains exactly two teams.

2. Each team always owns exactly four pieces.

3. A game may contain two, three, or four players.

4. Every player belongs to exactly one team.

5. Every piece belongs to exactly one player.

6. Every piece also belongs to exactly one team.

------------------------------------------------------------
Movement Rules
------------------------------------------------------------

7. Every move selected by an AI must come from the legal move list generated by the game engine.

8. The AI must never create its own moves.

9. The AI must never modify the legal move list.

10. A normal move must contain:
- moving piece ID
- move value
- destination progress

11. A special X-stick capture must contain:
- moving piece ID
- target piece ID

------------------------------------------------------------
Game Engine Responsibility
------------------------------------------------------------

12. Only the game engine may:
- move pieces
- update the board
- perform captures
- perform stacking
- change turns
- award extra turns
- detect a winner
- finish the game

13. AI strategies only choose one legal move.

14. AI strategies never change the game state directly.

------------------------------------------------------------
Random Easy Strategy
------------------------------------------------------------

15. Random Easy chooses exactly one legal move.

16. Every legal move has an equal chance of being selected.

17. Random Easy rejects an empty legal move list.

18. Random Easy always returns one of the supplied legal moves.

------------------------------------------------------------
Testing
------------------------------------------------------------

19. Every GameMove returned by an AI must pass validation.

20. Random Easy must support deterministic testing by allowing a seeded Random object.

21. Automated tests verify that:
- only legal moves are returned
- empty move lists are rejected
- both normal moves and X-stick captures are supported
- the legal move list is not modified
- repeated selections can produce different legal moves

------------------------------------------------------------
Future AI Strategies
------------------------------------------------------------

22. Greedy, Heuristic, and LLM strategies must satisfy every invariant listed above.

23. The only difference between AI strategies is how they choose from the legal move list.

24. All rule enforcement remains inside the game engine regardless of which AI strategy is used.
