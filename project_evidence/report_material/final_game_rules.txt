Players:
4

Teams:
Team 1 = Player 1 and Player 3
Team 2 = Player 2 and Player 4

Turn Order:
Player 1, Player 2, Player 3, Player 4, then repeat

Board:
29 spaces

Pieces:
Use the number of pieces already required by the official Game B assignment.

Throw Rules:
Each bright side counts as 1.
Each dark side counts as 0.
Four dark sides equal a move value of 5.
Four bright sides equal a move value of 4 and grant an extra throw.

Movement:
A piece must move the exact value of the throw.

Capturing:
Only opposing-team pieces may be captured.
Captured pieces return to the starting space.

Stacking:
Stacking must follow the official Game B rule already used in the project.

Winning:
A team wins when all required pieces belonging to both players on that team finish.

No Legal Move:
The turn advances according to the existing engine rules.

Automated Game Limit:
A game stops after 500 turns and is recorded as a timeout if neither team wins.
